=== CHAPTER 7: QUANTUM COMPUTING & SUPERPOSITION ===
Author: Institute of Advanced Physics

1. Fundamental Unit: The Qubit
While a classical bit exists strictly in state |0> or |1>, a quantum qubit exists in a linear superposition:
|psi> = alpha|0> + beta|1>, where |alpha|^2 + |beta|^2 = 1.
The probabilities of measuring 0 or 1 correspond to |alpha|^2 and |beta|^2 respectively.

2. Quantum Entanglement & Bell States
Two qubits are entangled when their composite quantum state cannot be factored into product states of individual qubits.
The canonical Bell State |Phi+> = (1 / sqrt(2)) * (|00> + |11>).
Measuring the first qubit immediately collapses the second qubit, regardless of spatial separation.

3. Quantum Gates
- Hadamard Gate (H): Maps basis states |0> and |1> into equal superposition states.
- Pauli-X (NOT gate): Flips |0> to |1> and vice versa.
- CNOT (Controlled-NOT): Entangles two qubits based on control state.

4. No-Cloning Theorem
An unknown arbitrary quantum state cannot be duplicated identically without destroying the original state.
