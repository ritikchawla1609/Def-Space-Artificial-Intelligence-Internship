1. Oral Defense Viva Voce (`/viva thermo-notes.pdf`)

Raw Prompting (Baseline)
• Dumps all 5 questions simultaneously in a giant numbered wall of text.
• Fails to wait for student responses before revealing answers.
• Gives superficial "Good job!" or "Incorrect" without probing physical laws.
• Destroys the authentic oral examination dynamic.
✅ Custom Skill (`/viva`)
• Strict turn-by-turn question delivery (one question at a time).
• Nuanced diagnostic critique ("Partly... but what about an ideal one?").
